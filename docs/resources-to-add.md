## Digitunity
- **Category:** Technology / Device Access (digital inclusion)
- **What it is:** National nonprofit that connects surplus technology (laptops, desktops, tablets) from donors to nonprofits and the communities they serve; works to close the digital divide at scale.
- **Why list it:** A concrete "resource you can access without a grant" — nonprofits on Anansi Atlas can source devices for the people they serve.
- **Website:** https://digitunity.org
- **Contact (from Aeras Foundation relationship):** Meredith Wynne — meredith@digitunity.org
- **Suggested resource tags:** Technology, Equipment, Shared services, Digital inclusion
- **Status:** ☐ Add to site Resources directory
