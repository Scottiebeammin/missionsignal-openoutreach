# Anansi Atlas — Overview

**Tagline:** The Web of Opportunity — built around your mission.
**One-liner:** The intelligence layer that maps every funder, partner, and pathway aligned to a nonprofit's mission — pulled live, ranked by fit, ready to act on.

## What it does
A nonprofit completes a ~10-minute plain-language intake (mission, geography, programs, stage) and receives the **Opportunity Web Snapshot** — an executive brief with:
- Ranked top funder matches (mission + geographic fit)
- Strategic partners
- Active grants with real deadlines and fit analysis
- **Readiness Score (0–100)** with named gaps
- **30-day action plan**
- Government funding pathways (federal / state / local)

Built for executive directors and program leads — not development specialists.

## The moat
- **Owned pipeline, not a resold database.** Live ingestion from primary sources (Grants.gov API + local/county/state/national/corporate/bank funders).
- **Grounding gate:** every opportunity must trace to a verifiable source URL or it's rejected — zero hallucination.
- **Cost:** cents per org vs. $2k–$4k/yr per-seat licenses incumbents resell.

## Market
- ~1.8M US nonprofits; focus on the underserved **$250K–$5M budget band**.
- $3T+ sector assets, $600B+ annual revenue.

## Business model
- **Founding Partner:** $150/mo, locked for life (19 seats left per deck) + 45-min founder walkthrough.
- **Standard:** $299/mo after founding cohort fills.
- Future: team seats, white-label licensing for capacity builders/consultants/funders.

## Traction
- Live platform (intake, snapshot, workspace operational).
- 60+ funders mapped; verified opportunities pulled per org.
- $0 outside funding — default-alive.

## Tech context (this repo)
- **Hermes** — research engine pulling live verified funders/partners/opportunities into the DB.
- **OpenOutreach** — self-hosted LinkedIn + email outreach automation (lead gen).
- Domains: anansiatlas.com, mail.anansiatlas.com.
